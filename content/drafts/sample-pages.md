**Three ways to avoid getting cheated**
1. Never pay anyone on UPI for "early" or "VIP" tickets.
2. Only buy from the official link — check the website address carefully.
3. If it sounds too good, it's a scam. Report it to cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

**Distances:** 🏟 ~__ km to stadium · 🛕 ~__ km to Kashi Vishwanath · 🚆 ~__ km to Cantt

**Description**
A small, family-run place two minutes' walk from Assi Ghat — close enough that you'll hear the morning aarti from the terrace. Rooms are simple and clean; the ones on the top floor have a partial river view and cost a little more.

It's a good fit if the match is one part of a longer Kashi trip and you want to be near the ghats in the evenings. It's not the best pick if you're only here for the cricket: the drive to Ganjari from Assi is longer than from Cantt, and the lane outside is too narrow for cars, so your cab will pick you up from the main road (about a 3-minute walk).

When we called in October, the owner said they can arrange an early breakfast on match days if you ask the night before. Check-in is from 12 pm; they're flexible if the room is free.
