Makar Sankranti is one of the busiest bathing days of the year in Varanasi. In 2026, lakhs of people took a dip in the Ganga at the city's ghats, gave til, jaggery and rice as daan, and shared khichdi. Kite flying is part of the day too.

Here's what you need to plan a visit for 2027: the date and snan timings, which ghats get crowded, darshan, road closures, kite safety and January weather.

## When is Makar Sankranti 2027?

**Friday, 15 January 2027.** The Sun enters Makara (Capricorn) at **9:14 pm on Thursday, 14 January**. That's after sunset, so the punya kaal, the auspicious window for the holy dip and daan, moves to the next morning.

| Varanasi, 15 Jan 2027 | Time |
|---|---|
| Maha Punya Kaal (most auspicious) | 6:45 am – 8:33 am |
| Punya Kaal | 6:45 am – 5:29 pm |

*Timings from Drik Panchang for Varanasi. Local pandits and calendars can differ by a few minutes.*

In 2026, the Sankranti moment fell on the afternoon of 14 January, but most people in Varanasi still bathed on the 15th. In 2027 there's no such confusion, since every major calendar we checked gives the 15th.

## What happens in Varanasi on Makar Sankranti

![Til ke laddu, sesame and jaggery sweets made for Makar Sankranti](/images/festivals/makar-sankranti/til-ke-laddu-makar-sankranti.webp)

- **Ganga snan.** In 2026, Dashashwamedh, Panchganga and Assi were the main bathing ghats, and Namo Ghat was crowded too.
- **Daan and khichdi.** Til, jaggery, rice and dal are given as daan. In 2026, khichdi prasad was handed out at [Manikarnika Ghat](/ghats/manikarnika-ghat/).
- **Kashi Vishwanath.** In 2026 the temple offered a special sattvik bhog of khichdi, chivda, peanut brittle and papad, followed by the midday bhog aarti.
- **Kites.** In January 2026, the city held a kite-flying contest on the sand bank across the river from the ghats, opened by the mayor.

![People flying kites from the rooftops of Varanasi on a hazy winter evening](/images/festivals/makar-sankranti/rooftop-kite-flying-varanasi-winter.webp)

## Where to go for the holy dip

- **[Dashashwamedh Ghat](/ghats/dashashwamedh-ghat/)** is the busiest and the easiest to find, but expect a slow walk in and out.
- **[Assi Ghat](/ghats/assi-ghat/)** is in the south and is a little easier to reach by road.
- **Panchganga Ghat** in the north was one of the main bathing ghats in 2026.

![Two women praying and taking a dip at the edge of the Ganga on the ghat steps in Varanasi](/images/festivals/makar-sankranti/morning-bathers-at-varanasi-ghats.webp)

**Tips for the snan:**

- **Arrive before 6:45 am** if you want to bathe in the Maha Punya Kaal. The last stretch to the ghat is on foot (see road closures below).
- **It's cold.** January mornings in Varanasi average about 9°C. Carry a towel, a full change of clothes and a warm layer for afterwards.
- **The steps get wet and slippery** near the water. Stay inside the area marked by the police and boatmen, and keep children close.
- **Keep valuables to a minimum.** Crowds are dense and you'll be leaving things on the steps while you bathe.

## Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath

Expect long queues. Rules can change at short notice in peak periods. In January 2025, sparsh darshan (touching the lingam) was stopped until late February because of the Maha Kumbh crowds, and only jhanki darshan from a distance was allowed. For the 2026 New Year rush, sparsh darshan was paused until 3 January.

The temple trust announces arrangements for 2027 closer to the date. We'll update this page then. For regular timings and Sugam Darshan, see our [Kashi Vishwanath guide](/temples/kashi-vishwanath/).

## Road closures and getting to the ghats

The old city around the ghats is closed to traffic when crowds are big. In January 2026, the police made the **Maidagin–Godowlia road a no-vehicle zone** for the Magh Mela crowds. No cars, autos or VIP vehicles were allowed, and golf carts carried elderly, disabled and pregnant visitors.

Plan for something similar in 2027:

- Take a cab or auto as far as you can, then **walk the last kilometre or so** to Dashashwamedh.
- Leave much more time than usual, and pick a meeting point in case your group gets separated.

**Magh Mela adds to the crowds.** Makar Sankranti is also the first day of the Magh Mela at Prayagraj, which runs **15 January to 6 March 2027**. Prayagraj is about 121 km away, and many pilgrims visit both cities. The 2026 closures above were put in place for exactly these Magh Mela crowds.

## Kite flying: the manjha warning

![Spools of kite string and kites for sale on a Varanasi street in January](/images/festivals/makar-sankranti/kite-string-spools-varanasi-january.webp)

Kite flying is the fun part of the day, but sharp **nylon or "Chinese" manjha** can cause deep cuts and has killed people, including riders on two-wheelers caught by loose thread.

- Nylon and synthetic manjha has been **banned across India since 2017**. The Allahabad High Court has repeatedly told the UP government to enforce the ban during kite season.
- In January 2026, a physiotherapist in nearby Jaunpur was killed by Chinese manjha.
- **If you fly kites, use cotton thread.** If you're on a two-wheeler, ride slowly, wrap a scarf around your neck and watch for loose thread across the road.

## January weather and travel

![A boat on the Ganga beside the Varanasi ghats in January](/images/festivals/makar-sankranti/varanasi-ghats-in-january.webp)

- **Cold and foggy.** IMD's long-term average for Varanasi in January is about 9°C at night and 22°C by day, with fog on about 22 days of the month.
- **Fog delays flights and trains.** On 6 January 2026, fog affected 26 flights at Babatpur airport, with some more than four hours late. Trains through Varanasi also run hours late in thick fog, as in January 2023.
- **Give yourself a buffer.** If you have to be at the ghat at dawn on the 15th, arrive on the 14th.

## Where to stay

To be at the ghat for the 6:45 am window without a pre-dawn drive through fog, stay walking distance from the river: around [Dashashwamedh](/areas/dashashwamedh/), [Assi](/areas/assi/) or [Bengali Tola](/areas/bengali-tola/). Areas like Cantt or Lanka are better for trains and roads, but put you behind the road closures on the morning itself.
